Data deletion

Mote Explains · effective 16 October 2026

The Mote Explains app keeps its data only on the owner's personal computer (see the privacy policy).

To have your data deleted

  1. Email moteexplains@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your Instagram or YouTube username so the records can be found.
  3. Every stored record about your account (public post metrics, profile counts) is deleted within 30 days, and you get a confirmation email.

If you connected your own account to the app

Only the channel owner's accounts are connected. To remove the app's access to an Instagram account: Instagram app → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → remove it. Its token stops working at once, and the stored data is deleted on request as above.
