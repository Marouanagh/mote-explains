Privacy policy

Mote Explains · effective 16 October 2026

Mote Explains is a one-person creator channel. Its owner runs a small private tool (the "app") on his own computer to read analytics for the channel's own YouTube and Instagram accounts and public information that helps choose topics for new videos. The app has no other users, shows no ads, and is not offered to anyone.

What the app reads

  • The channel's own accounts (@moteexplains on Instagram, @mote.explains on YouTube): public post metrics and insights such as views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time and follower counts, through the platforms' official APIs, with the owner's permission.
  • Public information about other professional accounts on Instagram, through Meta's Business Discovery: profile name, follower count, and public posts' captions, type, date, like, comment and view counts and links.
  • Public hashtag results on Instagram (if approved by Meta): aggregated counts and topic labels for a short list of science hashtags.
  • Public videos and feeds from YouTube and other official public feeds (news, Wikipedia, space-news services).

The app does not read private messages, does not collect personal data about private individuals, does not download other creators' videos, and does not post, comment, like or follow anything.

How it is used

Only to understand how the channel's own videos perform and which space and physics topics people are curious about, so the owner can decide what original video to make next.

Where it is stored and for how long

On the owner's personal computer only. Access tokens are kept in a protected local folder and never shared. Analytics history is kept while the channel is active; public data about other accounts is kept for at most 12 months.

Sharing

Data is never sold, licensed, published or shared with third parties, and is not used to train machine-learning models. Summaries may be processed by AI assistants the owner uses to write his own notes; no data is published.

Your choices

To ask what is stored about your account, or to have it deleted, see the data deletion page or email moteexplains@gmail.com.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
